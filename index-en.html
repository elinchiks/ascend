Minska was the first modern shopping center in Riga. Residents of Purvciems take particular pride in the fact that almost everything they need is within easy reach in their neighborhood. As part of an art project, every shop located within what was once the most modern shopping center in the Baltics was documented.

As part of the art project, photographs were taken of Bolt and Wolt delivery workers who preferred to remain anonymous by covering their faces with masks.

This art project sought to document the residents of a single apartment building and capture their views on the future of their homes and neighborhood. The project focused on residents of Purvciems living in a Khrushchyovka, a Soviet era apartment block built in 1972.
